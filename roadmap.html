Why this order

The hardest step is the one that isn't a feature.

Step six is the first time an employer needs an actual account instead of just showing up in public filing data. Everything before it is a student looking things up. Everything from there on is two people making a decision together, so it gets built last, on purpose.

  • 01

    Public data first

    Visa filings and OPT hiring history come from federal records that already exist. No partnership required to show them.

  • 02

    Then the school's own data

    Requirements, discounts, and in-network status come from the partner school directly, never guessed.

  • 03

    Then the student's own record

    Coursework, skills, and experience she's already entered, matched against a live role.

  • 04

    The employer, last

    The one party that needs its own login, its own screens, built once everything upstream of it is real.

Hiro is a pathway and career-planning tool for international students. Hiro is not an immigration attorney, a Designated School Official, or an educational institution, and nothing on this site or in the app is legal, immigration, or academic advice. Visa filing history, program requirements, and sponsorship data shown are drawn from public records and documented school requirements, never presented as a promise or a likelihood of any outcome. Confirm eligibility, deadlines, and requirements with your school's DSO and, where relevant, immigration counsel.
