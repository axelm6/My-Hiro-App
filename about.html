About

Built by someone who had to figure this out the hard way.

Hiro exists because the guidance a student actually needs, when to apply, who to ask, what happens after the certificate ends, was never written down anywhere in one place. It lived in advisors' heads, in forum threads, in luck.

The founder

I'm Axel Martinez. I lived the OPT and H-1B version of this journey myself, the uncertainty about timelines, the not knowing whether a company had ever sponsored anyone in my position, the advice that was really just a guess dressed up as confidence. Hiro is the tool I wish someone had handed me before I left home.

Building it with Wilmar Tarazona, who builds the product itself.

What we won't do

  • —

    Promise an outcome

    Not an admissions decision, not a visa approval, not a job offer.

  • —

    Guess at a requirement

    Every program requirement traces back to the school that actually publishes it.

  • —

    Act as your immigration counsel

    Hiro shows dates, costs, and documents. Your DSO and, where relevant, an attorney are the ones who advise.

Trust

  • Security & privacy

Hiro is a pathway and career-planning tool for international students. Hiro is not an immigration attorney, a Designated School Official, or an educational institution, and nothing on this site or in the app is legal, immigration, or academic advice. Visa filing history, program requirements, and sponsorship data shown are drawn from public records and documented school requirements, never presented as a promise or a likelihood of any outcome. Confirm eligibility, deadlines, and requirements with your school's DSO and, where relevant, immigration counsel.
